THE GYM RATS

Your personal fitness command center

Version 1.0
The Idea

Built for people who take fitness seriously.

Most fitness apps are bloated, subscription-locked, or require an internet connection just to log a workout. The Gym Rats was built differently — a single, fast, offline-first web app that lives in your browser. No accounts sent to servers. No monthly fees. No distractions. Just you and your data.

The idea started simple: one place to track workouts, supplements, gym membership, expenses, body progress, and challenges with your gym crew — all without leaving your device.

How It Works

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

01
Sign up & log in
Create an account with a username and password. Your credentials are stored only on this device using browser localStorage — no server ever sees them.
02
All data stays local
Every workout, supplement, expense, and progress entry is saved to your browser's localStorage under a single key. It persists across sessions until you clear it.
03
Export & import backups
Use Settings → Export to download a full JSON backup of your data. Import it back anytime on any device to restore everything instantly.
04
Creator controls
The creator account has access to a special dashboard — manage all registered users, toggle maintenance mode, and control app availability.
How to Use

Six modules. One dashboard.

Workouts
Log every session with exercises, sets, reps, and weight. The app automatically detects personal records and tracks your streak. View full history and PRs anytime.
Supplements
Add your supplements with quantity, serving size, and daily dose. Hit "Take Scoop" each day and the app tracks remaining inventory, estimates days left, and alerts you when running low.
Membership
Save your gym membership details — gym name, fee, start and end dates. The dashboard shows days remaining with a live progress bar and alerts you before it expires.
Expenses
Log every fitness-related expense — gym fees, supplements, equipment. Split costs with gym partners and track who has paid and who owes. Monthly totals shown on the dashboard.
Progress
Log body metrics — weight, body fat, chest, waist, arms, legs. Charts visualize your transformation over time. Every entry is timestamped and stored in your progress log.
Challenges
Create fitness challenges — workout count goals, streak targets, or weight goals. Set a deadline and track progress. Add friends and run a leaderboard to compete and stay motivated.
